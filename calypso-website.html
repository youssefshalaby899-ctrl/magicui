Our story

Not just a table.
A place to land.

Calypso began with a simple idea: Cairo needed a room that treats you like a guest in someone's home. Good food, honest drinks, shisha prepared with care — and staff who remember your name by the second visit.

Find us on El Kafrawi Axis, where the terrace catches the evening breeze and dinner has a way of turning into the whole night.
