The Room

Not a barbershop.
A half‑hour that
belongs to you.

ONE GOLDEN SQUARE
NEW CAIRO, EGYPT

Mankind started with two chairs, one mirror and a rule we have never broken: nobody leaves in a hurry. We cut dry, we cut slow, and we cut to the shape of your head rather than the picture on your phone — though we will get you close to that too.

Every barber on the floor is trained in-house on the same three things: a clean baseline, a fade that disappears rather than bands, and a finish you can see from across a room. Then we hand you a hot towel, a mirror, and the fifteen minutes most places skip.

The room itself is deliberately quiet — low brass light, wood, and a record playing at conversation volume. Bring a friend, wait with a coffee, or say nothing at all. Both are entirely acceptable here.

The Ritual

Forty‑five minutes,
in order

Nothing here is improvised. The sequence is the service — skip a step and you have a haircut, not a Mankind cut.

01

Five minutes of talking

Before anything is picked up. Growth pattern, cowlicks, how often you actually come back, and what you do with it on a normal Tuesday morning — because that is the haircut we have to build.

Consultation · no clippers yet

02

Dry work first

Hair lies differently wet. We establish the shape and the baseline dry, where we can see exactly what your head does with it, then go back with water for refinement.

Structure · baseline · weight

03

The blend

Three passes, checked in the hand mirror against the wall mirror. A fade either disappears or it is a mistake — we are not interested in the middle outcome.

Guideline removal · two‑mirror check

04

Edge & steam

Razor along the hairline and neck, fresh blade, with the grain. Then a hot towel pressed and held — not waved — until the skin stops fighting.

Line‑up · hot towel · cold close

05

Finish & the mirror turn

Product matched to your hair, not to a shelf. Then we hand you the mirror and show you how to get the same result in three minutes at home — including the part you will always forget.

Blow‑dry · product · aftercare
